Debt Freedom v5 — two-stage payday routine

ACCOUNT ROLES
Regular:
- Pay is deposited here.
- Automatic debt withdrawals come from here.
- Manual bills/debt payments are made here.
- Keep a $200 permanent buffer.

Rent:
- Rent and car insurance.
- Planned-expense savings.
- Emergency savings.
- Debt-payment reserve.

Scotia:
- Spending only: groceries, gas and personal/fun.

PAYDAY
1. Transfer groceries + gas + personal spending to Scotia.
2. Transfer rent savings + car-insurance amount to Rent.
3. Leave everything else in Regular overnight.

NEXT MORNING
1. Wait until overnight automatic withdrawals clear.
2. Enter the REAL Regular-account balance.
3. The app protects the $200 Regular buffer and any later automatic bills.
4. Pay manual bills.
5. Transfer planned/emergency savings to Rent.
6. Make a past-due payment only when it is a meaningful chunk (about 1/3, 1/2 or full).
7. Any unused debt money is protected in Rent as debt reserve.

Privacy:
- This public app package contains no personal balances.
- Never upload the private plan JSON to GitHub.
